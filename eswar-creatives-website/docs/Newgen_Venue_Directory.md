## Name discrepancies to settle

Our project documents and Google do not agree on three names. Google is the public
name a client will recognise, so it probably wins for anything client-facing, but the
partnership timeline on the website currently uses the other spellings.

| Our documents | Google | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Sri Venkatesh Mahal | Shree Venkatesh Mahal | Newgen's longest partnership, since 2017 |
| Selvi Mahal | Selvi Thirumana Mahal | See the disambiguation warning below |
| MKS Grand Palace | M.K.S Grand Palace | Punctuation only |

**Selvi needs care.** Google's "people also search for" panel on that listing shows two
other Chennai venues literally named "Selvi Mahal", one rated 4.1 with 343 reviews and
one rated 4.0 with 964 reviews, plus a "Sri Udaya Selvi Palace". Newgen's partner is
**Selvi Thirumana Mahal** at Keelkattalai. Writing "Selvi Mahal" anywhere client-facing
points at the wrong business, and the two others have more reviews so they rank higher.

## Ratings, and a caution

Illam at 4.9 with 1,379 reviews is a genuinely strong number and is the venue that won
Times Hospitality Icons 2025.

Do not conflate any of these with Newgen's own rating. The verified stats table in the
project context has Newgen at 4.9 from 26 reviews. That is Newgen's listing. These are
the venues' listings and belong to the venues, not to Newgen.
