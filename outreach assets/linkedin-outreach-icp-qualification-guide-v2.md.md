## Ideal Client Profile — Design Systems for B2B SaaS

### Primary ICP (highest conversion probability)

**Company profile**

- B2B SaaS, Series A to Series C
- 15 to 150 engineers
- US, UK, or EU market
- Shipping across 2 or more platforms (web plus mobile, or web plus desktop)
- Dashboard-heavy, workflow-heavy, or data-heavy product
- Verticals: security, AI/ML, fintech, HR tech, devtools, healthtech

**The moment they become a buyer**
One or more of these is true:

- Engineers are rebuilding the same component in multiple places
- Design and engineering are arguing about what "correct" looks like
- A new platform (iOS, Android, desktop) is being added and the team dreads inconsistency
- A rebrand or product redesign is planned and the team knows the current system will not survive it
- The team just hired their 3rd or 4th designer and has no shared foundation
- A CPO or VP Product joined and their first audit found inconsistency everywhere
- An enterprise customer complained about the product feeling "unpolished"

**Buyer title (who signs)**

- CPO (Chief Product Officer) — most common
- Co-Founder/CEO at early stage (15 to 40 engineers)
- VP Product or VP Engineering at mid-stage
- Head of Design (rare, usually needs CPO approval)

**Budget signal**

- Series A: $5K to $15K for a scoped system
- Series B/C: $15K to $50K for full token architecture plus component library
- Wedge entry: $750 UX Audit (credited toward full engagement)

---

### Secondary ICP (slower to close, still worth targeting)

**Company profile**

- Pre-Series A with strong traction and funded engineering team
- Enterprise software company spinning up a new product line
- Agency or consultancy building products for multiple clients (needs a system they can reuse)
- Indian startup with US/EU ambitions (bridge market for you)

---

### Anti-ICP (do not spend time here)

- Consumer apps (B2C, social, gaming)
- E-commerce or retail
- Agencies that want design execution, not systems
- Companies with fewer than 5 engineers
- Founders without funding (no budget)
- Single-platform products with no mobile plans
- Companies where the design buyer is a junior designer (no authority)
- Restaurant tech, fashion tech, education content (too far from your proof points)

### How to qualify a lead in 60 seconds

Open their site and check these 4 things:

| Signal | Green | Red |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product type | Dashboard, workflow, data | Landing page only, consumer app |
| Platform | Web plus mobile mentioned | Web only, no mobile |
| Stage | Series A to C, funded | Bootstrapped, pre-revenue |
| Buyer title | CPO, CEO, VP Product | Designer, PM, marketing |

If 3 of 4 are green, add to portal. If 2 or fewer, skip.
