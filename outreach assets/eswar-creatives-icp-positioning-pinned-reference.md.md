### 5-Question Qualification Checklist

- [ ]  Series A–C, B2B SaaS, 30–200 employees?
- [ ]  2–6 designers with no dedicated DS owner?
- [ ]  Shipping across 2+ platforms (or planning to)?
- [ ]  Trigger event present — funding / new platform / design departure / dev friction?
- [ ]  Economic buyer is Head of Design, VP Product, or CTO?

**3+ yes → book discovery call. Fewer than 3 → qualify out early.**

## Part 2.2 — Positioning Statement

> *For scaling B2B SaaS teams that ship across multiple platforms, Eswar Creatives is the design systems specialist who delivers a production-ready token architecture and component library — without the cost and ramp time of a full-time hire.*
> 

### The Wedge

Lead with the narrowest true thing: **“Design systems architect for scaling B2B SaaS”** — not “UX consultant.” Narrow wins because the buyer self-identifies instantly and stops comparing you to generalists.

### Competitive Edge

| vs. | Your edge |
| --- | --- |
| Generalist freelancer | Systems depth: tokens, components, cross-platform |
| UX agency | Senior IC. Direct ownership. No account managers |
| Full-time DS hire | Faster (weeks not months). 60–70% lower cost |
| Offshore teams | HFI-CUA certified. Enterprise SaaS track record |

## Trigger Events — Watch For These

- **Funding round** — just raised Series A/B, hiring designers fast
- **New surface** — launching 2nd product or mobile app
- **Dev friction** — engineering complaining about inconsistent handoff
- **Team growth** — design team doubled, UI visibly fragmenting
- **Design departure** — senior designer or design lead just left
- **Enterprise push** — customers asking for design maturity

## Objection Responses

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| *We’ll hire full-time* | A full-time DS hire is $150K+/yr + 3 months to ramp. I can have your system ready in 8 weeks. |
| *Can you work hourly?* | I price by outcome — you know the cost upfront and I’m accountable to delivery, not time. |
| *You’re in Chennai* | All CYGNVS work was remote with a US/EU team. Timezone is a feature, not a risk. |
| *We need a generalist* | Specialists ship faster. I’ve already built one — a generalist builds a DS after figuring it out. |
